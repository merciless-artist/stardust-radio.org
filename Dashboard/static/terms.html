Stardust Radio — Terms of Service

Effective: August 7, 2026

Stardust Radio is a community radio project: a web radio station, a Discord bot, a listening-party dashboard, and a Discord Activity. Using any of them means you're okay with these terms. We've kept them short and human.

1. What Stardust Radio is

A free, community-run service for sharing and listening to music together — including songs made with AI tools like Suno. It's a hobby project run by real people, not a company.

2. Your music

  • Submitting a song gives us permission to broadcast it on the station — nothing more. You keep all your rights.
  • You can withdraw a song anytime by asking in the support server; we'll take it down and delete the data.
  • Hosts review submissions and can decline or remove any song for any reason.

3. Be decent

Don't use the service to submit or share anything illegal, hateful, or intended to harm or harass. Don't try to break, overload, or sneak around the service's access rules. Follow Discord's Terms wherever the bot or Activity is involved.

5. Changes and ending things
