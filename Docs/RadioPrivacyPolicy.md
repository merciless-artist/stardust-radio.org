Stardust Radio is a community listening-party tool: a web dashboard for hosting and queueing music during listening parties with a helper bot that is invited to the server. Invite the discord helper bot with this link: 

[Stardust Radio Helper Bot](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1503047790496977067)

Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, username/display name, and the server (guild) and channel ID where the bot is active.

Songs you submit: music links you post in a tracked channel (Suno, YouTube, SoundCloud, etc.), the platform, and a timestamp.

Votes/reactions you cast on songs during a party.

Message content -- only in channels where tracking is turned on, and only to detect music links. We do not store messages or read messages, the bot runs an algorithm to detect music links only.

For hosts (people who sign into the Booth): we use Discord sign-in (OAuth) to confirm your identity and your "DJ" role; we store a login session cookie.

Voice/stage membership: when an admin uses the `!vcping` command, the bot reads who is currently in that voice/stage channel in order to mention them, but does not save the mentioned users.

## Why we collect it

Solely to run listening parties to track submitted songs, build and display the queue, credit songs to whoever submitted them, tally votes, and let hosts manage the session.

## Where it's stored & who can see it

Data is stored in a private database controlled by the operator. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Submitted songs and your attribution are visible to others in your server and on the screen-shared dash during a party. The links are deleted from the database upon the ending of a listening party, when the host uses the command !stoptracking in the discord channel. 

## Third parties

Discord -- we use Discord's API and sign-in; your use is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.
Cloudflare -- serves/secures the dashboard.
Suno / YouTube / SoundCloud -- when showing a song's title, art, or lyrics, we fetch that song's public page from these services. We don't send them your personal info.

## Retention & your choices

Tracked songs for a channel are deleted when a host runs `!stoptracking`, or on request.

You can ask us to delete your song from the dash during an LP at any time, or wait until it is deleted at the end of the LP.

Don't want a link tracked? Don't post it in a tracked channel.

## Children

The service isn't directed at anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), consistent with Discord's Terms.

## Changes

We may update this policy; the "Effective date" will change. Material changes will be announced in the community.
