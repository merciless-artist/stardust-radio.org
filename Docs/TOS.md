 1. What the Service Is



Stardust Radio is a continuous internet radio station. It plays a catalog of songs that have been explicitly submitted to us with consent to broadcast on the radio. We do not stream songs we do not have permission for. If a song is on the radio, the rights-holder (typically an independent artist or creator) handed it to us specifically for this purpose.



The Dashboard is a separate web page used to host time-bounded "listening parties." During a party, songs submitted via Discord queue commands are played back through the dashboard for participants. Listening-party songs are not broadcast on the radio station, are not stored after the party, and are deleted from the queue when the party ends. The dashboard also embeds a Discord chat widget so participants can talk during the event.



The radio station and the dashboard are operated by the same person but are technically independent. One does not feed the other.
